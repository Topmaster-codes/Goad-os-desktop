# Goad-OS Branding

The code and assets in this repository are open source (see [README](README.md#license--open-source)).
Anyone can use, modify, and redistribute them. The **Goad-OS branding** is the one
thing that is not part of that grant.

This is a plain-language policy, not legal advice.

## What counts as branding

- The name "Goad-OS" (and spellings such as "GoaDOS"), and names that are confusingly similar.
- The Goad-OS logo and official visual identity, including the ASCII-art logo in
  [`fastfetch/goad-os.txt`](fastfetch/goad-os.txt).

## What you can do

- Fork the project, change it, and redistribute it under the licenses in the README.
- Say that your project is "based on" or "a fork of" Goad-OS, and link back to this repository.
- Describe compatibility, for example "works with Goad-OS configs".

## What you shouldn't do

- Name your project or distribution Goad-OS, or something easily mistaken for it.
- Use the Goad-OS name, logo, or visual identity as the identity of your own project.
- Suggest that your version is the official Goad-OS or is endorsed by its author.

If you publish a modified version, please give it its own name and replace the logo
(for example, `fastfetch/goad-os.txt`) with your own.

## Other projects' names

CachyOS, Arch Linux, Hyprland, Amane, Rofi, and other names mentioned in this repository
belong to their respective owners. Goad-OS is not affiliated with or endorsed by them.
